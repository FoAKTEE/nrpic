\section{Relativistic kinetic system in 3+1 form}\label{sec:system}

Consider massive particles of rest mass $\mu_p$ moving along timelike trajectories on the future mass shell $g^{\mu\nu} u_\mu u_\nu = -1$, where $u_\mu$ denotes covariant four-velocity per unit rest mass. Under a three-plus-one Arnowitt-Deser-Misner (ADM) foliation \cite{Arnowitt:1962hi} of spacelike hypersurfaces $\Sigma_t$ with lapse $\alpha$, shift vector $\beta^i$, induced spatial three-metric $\gamma_{ij}$, inverse $\gamma^{ij}$, and determinant $\gamma \equiv \det(\gamma_{ij})$, the spacetime line element reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adm_metric}
\dd s^2 = -\alpha^2\,\dd t^2 + \gamma_{ij}(\dd x^i + \beta^i\,\dd t)(\dd x^j + \beta^j\,\dd t).
\end{equation}
Normal observers pierce $\Sigma_t$ orthogonally along $n_\mu = (-\alpha, 0, 0, 0)$ and $n^\mu = (\alpha^{-1}, -\alpha^{-1}\beta^i)$, measuring the Lorentz factor
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lorentz_w}
W = -n^\mu u_\mu = \sqrt{1 + \gamma^{ij} u_i u_j},
\end{equation}
and the raised spatial components $u^i = \gamma^{ij} u_j$. In canonical Darboux phase-space coordinates $(x^i, u_i)$, the single-particle Hamiltonian equals the coordinate energy $-u_0 = \alpha W - \beta^i u_i$ \cite{Acuna-Cardenas:2021nkj}. Hamilton's canonical equations yield the normal-observer characteristic equations,
\begin{align}\label{eq:characteristics}
\frac{\dd x^i}{\dd t} &= \frac{\partial H}{\partial u_i} = \frac{\alpha \gamma^{ij} u_j}{W} - \beta^i, \nonumber\\
\frac{\dd u_i}{\dd t} &= -\frac{\partial H}{\partial x^i} = -W \partial_i \alpha + u_j \partial_i \beta^j + \frac{\alpha}{2W} u^l u^m \partial_i \gamma_{lm}.
\end{align}

On the future mass shell, the Lorentz-invariant momentum measure is $\dd\Pi_u = \dd^3 u / (\sqrt{\gamma}\,W)$, while the proper spatial volume element on $\Sigma_t$ is $\sqrt{\gamma}\,\dd^3 x$. Their product coincides with the flat canonical Liouville measure $\dd^3 x\,\dd^3 u$. Defining the Darboux rest-mass phase-space density $f(t, \bm{x}, \bm{u}) \equiv \sqrt{\gamma}\,W\,\mathcal{F}$ from the scalar distribution function $\mathcal{F}$ absorbs every metric determinant and Lorentz factor. Because the Hamiltonian phase flow satisfies $\partial_{x^i}(\dd x^i/\dd t) + \partial_{u_i}(\dd u_i/\dd t) = 0$ identically, Boltzmann transport takes the flux-conservative Darboux Vlasov law
\begin{equation}\label{eq:darboux_vlasov}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial}{\partial x^i}\!\left(\frac{\dd x^i}{\dd t} f\right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial u_i}\!\left(\frac{\dd u_i}{\dd t} f\right) = \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}\right)_{\rm coll}.
\end{equation}
Unconstrained covariant momenta $\bm{u} \in \mathbb{R}^3$ maintain strictly subluminal three-velocities everywhere, and all gravitational source terms in the characteristic equations vanish globally in Minkowski coordinates.

Normal observers measure the coordinate-densitized rest-mass density $D = \sqrt{\gamma}\,\rho_0$ and stress-energy projections $(E, S_i, S_{ij})$ directly from moments of $f$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adm_sources}
\sqrt{\gamma}\begin{pmatrix} \rho_0 \\ E \\ S_i \\ S_{ij} \end{pmatrix} = \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ W \\ u_i \\ u_i u_j / W \end{pmatrix} f(t,\bm{x},\bm{u})\,\dd^3 u.
\end{equation}
Dividing these integrals by $\sqrt{\gamma}$ supplies the matter source terms to Einstein's field equations evolved in the constraint-damped Z4c formulation \cite{Bona:2003fj,Hilditch:2012fp,Gundlach:2005eh} under moving-puncture $1+\log$ slicing and Gamma-driver shift gauge \cite{Bona:1994dr,Campanelli:2005dd,Alcubierre:2002kk}. Integrating the zeroth moment over $\Sigma_t$ establishes total rest-mass conservation $M_0 = \int_{\Sigma_t} D\,\dd^3 x = \int f\,\dd^3 x\,\dd^3 u$, while the ADM energy identity reads $\int_{\Sigma_t} E\sqrt{\gamma}\,\dd^3 x = \int W f\,\dd^3 x\,\dd^3 u$.

Along any spacetime Killing vector $\xi^\mu$, collisionless geodesic motion conserves $\xi^\mu u_\mu$ exactly. In stationary, axisymmetric Kerr spacetime of mass $M$ and spin parameter $a$, time translation and axial rotation symmetries yield specific geodesic energy $\Egeo = -u_t = \alpha W - \beta^i u_i$ and axial angular momentum $L_z = u_\phi$, while separability of the Hamilton-Jacobi equation preserves the Carter constant \cite{Wu:2026nrpic}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:carter_q}
Q = u_\theta^2 + \cos^2\!\theta\!\left[a^2(1 - \Egeo^2) + \frac{L_z^2}{\sin^2\!\theta}\right],
\end{equation}
with total Carter invariant $K = Q + (L_z - a\Egeo)^2$.

When two-body elastic scattering with reduced hard-sphere cross section $\sigma/\mu_p > 0$ operates, the collisional operator drives $\mathcal{F}$ toward the local relativistic Maxwell-J{\"u}ttner equilibrium evaluated in a normal-observer orthonormal tetrad $u_{\hat{\mu}}$ \cite{Israel:1963,Synge:1957}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:maxwell_juttner}
\mathcal{F}_{\rm MJ}(u^{\hat{\mu}}) = \frac{n_{\rm com}}{4\pi \theta K_2(1/\theta)} \exp\!\left(\frac{U_{\rm cm}^{\hat{\mu}} u_{\hat{\mu}}}{\theta}\right),
\end{equation}
where $n_{\rm com}$ is the comoving number density, $U_{\rm cm}^{\hat{\mu}}$ is the bulk four-velocity, $\theta \equiv k_B T / \mu_p$ is the dimensionless temperature, and $K_n$ denotes modified Bessel functions of the second kind. The Synge gas equation of state closes the thermodynamic state via specific relativistic enthalpy $h_{\rm rel}(\theta)$, mean comoving Lorentz factor $\bar{W}_{\rm com}(\theta)$, and adiabatic index $\Gamma_{\rm ad}(\theta) \in [4/3, 5/3]$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:synge_eos}
\begin{aligned}
h_{\rm rel}(\theta) &= \frac{K_3(1/\theta)}{K_2(1/\theta)}, \qquad \bar{W}_{\rm com}(\theta) = h_{\rm rel}(\theta) - \theta, \\
\Gamma_{\rm ad}(\theta) &= 1 + \frac{1}{h_{\rm rel}'(\theta) - 1}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
In the collisionless limit $\sigma/\mu_p = 0$, Eq.~(\ref{eq:darboux_vlasov}) reduces to exact Hamiltonian phase-space conservation along geodesics.
