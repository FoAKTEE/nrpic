\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Relativistic kinetic matter governs diverse compact-object environments, from dense stellar clusters and tidal debris around Kerr black holes to neutrino transport and marginally collisional plasmas in gravitational collapse \cite{1985ApJ...298...34S,1985ApJ...298...58S,Andreasson:2011ng,Acuna-Cardenas:2021nkj}. Across these systems the dimensionless Knudsen number varies by many orders of magnitude. Cold collapse and dilute galactic nuclei stream freely along timelike geodesics with vanishing two-body scattering, whereas dense relativistic outflows and shock layers relax rapidly toward local Maxwell-J{\"u}ttner equilibrium. Bridging both limits inside a dynamical three-plus-one spacetime requires accurate phase-space transport without excessive sampling noise, grid diffusion, or runaway computational cost.

Two numerical paradigms tackle kinetic transport in general relativity from opposite directions. Particle-in-cell (PIC) methods sample six-dimensional phase space with finite-mass characteristics pushed along geodesics and deposited onto an adaptive spacetime mesh \cite{Wu:2026nrpic,Galishnikova:2025pfi,2026ApJS..283...27S}. Symplectic integration along individual characteristics preserves Killing invariants in stationary spacetimes and introduces zero momentum diffusion across neighbors. Finite Monte Carlo particle counts nonetheless seed Poisson sampling noise and spurious black hole spin unless large ensembles are evolved, while binary Monte Carlo collision pairing stalls under subcycling at high optical depths. Conversely, meshless Lagrangian-basis moving-finite-volume (MFM) closures discretize velocity space with realizable Gaussian or top-hat basis functions coupled by exact one-sided Riemann fluxes \cite{Hopkins:2026lbv,Hopkins:2015mfc}. Moving elements capture continuum shocks cleanly, yet warm shearing flows in curved geometry expose neighbor-cell gradient dispersion, tidal covariance broadening, and nearest-neighbor search overhead on graphics processors.

Here we formulate and benchmark a unified hybrid phase-space solver for the general-relativistic Boltzmann-Vlasov system inside AthenaK \cite{2026ApJS..283...27S,Zhu:2024utz}. For continuum and transitional kinetic flows, Lagrangian velocity bases evolve under safeguarded-Newton one-sided fluxes blended into a relativistic Synge HLLC solver \cite{Mignone:2005ns,Synge:1957}, complemented in packet mode by an exact four-momentum-conserving relativistic Cell-BGK relaxation operator \cite{Anderson:1974,Israel:1963}. For collisionless spacetimes, deterministic Stroud cubature \cite{Stroud:1971}, spherical Lebedev shells, and symmetry-exact quasi-Monte Carlo sampling initialize phase-space packets pushed by a fourth-order symplectic Gauss-Legendre integrator with three-level quadratic metric time interpolation, $C^2$-smooth six-node quintic Hermite spatial reconstruction, stage-accurate causal source extrapolation, multi-rate matter subcycling, and a self-adjoint harmonic flux-conservative deposit filter \cite{Wu:2026nrpic,Halton:1960}. Five key results emerge:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Eliminating neighbor-grid collapse under adaptive smoothing-length ratios, packing compact kernel pointer structures, and replacing Brent root searches with analytical Newton steps cuts continuum GPU runtime sharply while preserving exact finite-volume parity.
\item Across four decades of collisionality ($\sigma/\mu_p \in [0, 10^4]$), conservative Cell-BGK relaxation recovers exact free streaming and matches warm Taub shocks to rounding energy precision at $\mathcal{O}(1)$ cost per step, delivering a fiftyfold wall-clock speedup over subcycled Monte Carlo collisions.
\item Pairing symmetry-exact quasi-Monte Carlo initialization with fourth-order symplectic integration and zero-VRAM-overhead $C^2$ quintic Hermite reconstruction cuts Kerr $e=0.99$ energy drift by six orders of magnitude, breaks the $6$-level static-mesh energy error floor by $311\times$ down to $|\Delta \Egeo/\Egeo|_{\rm med} = 3.38 \times 10^{-10}$, and holds linear momentum to $10^{-14}$ while suppressing spurious black hole spin twentyfold in collapsing star clusters.
\item Self-adjoint harmonic metric-weighted refluxing preserves constant physical fields and proper-volume ADM integrals to $\le 10^{-15}$ on curved adaptive meshes, while an unsplit seven-point harmonic formulation guarantees unconditional pointwise positivity for $\kappa \le 1/12$, cuts metric-conservative filter communication sweeps threefold, and yields a $3.19\times$ wall-clock speedup on $6$-level Kerr SMR clusters.
\item Causal stage-accurate Z4c source extrapolation removes $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$ matter-freezing error ($15\text{--}23\times$ smaller central-lapse error in Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse), enabling stable three-stage \texttt{rk3} evolution at high Courant numbers ($\nu_{\rm CFL}=0.40\text{--}0.45$) and continuous multi-rate matter subcycling (\texttt{matter\_stride}) with $2.24\text{--}2.75\times$ end-to-end GPU speedups across dynamical Oppenheimer-Snyder, Shapiro-Teukolsky, and Jeans binary collapses.
\end{enumerate}
