\section{Cubature parameters and testbed configurations}\label{app:params}

Each populated velocity basis $(a,\alpha)$ with positive rest mass $m_{a,\alpha} > 0$ expands into $Q_{\rm SCP}$ deterministic characteristic packets via the cubature packet expansion using the lower triangular Cholesky factor $\bm{L}_{a,\alpha}$ of the local velocity covariance $\Sigma_{a,\alpha}$ and an orthogonal rotation $\bm{R}_{a,\alpha} \in \mathrm{SO}(3)$ mapped from the base-$2,3,5$ Halton triplet $(h_2, h_3, h_5)$ through Shoemake's uniform unit quaternion
\begin{multline}\label{eq:shoemake_q}
\bm{q} = \big(\sqrt{1-h_2}\sin 2\pi h_3,\;\sqrt{1-h_2}\cos 2\pi h_3,\\
\sqrt{h_2}\sin 2\pi h_5,\;\sqrt{h_2}\cos 2\pi h_5\big).
\end{multline}
Cold elements with $\mathrm{Tr}\,\Sigma_{a,\alpha} = 0$ collapse automatically to $Q_{\rm SCP} = 1$ packet ($\bm{\xi}_1 = \bm{0}$, $w_1 = 1$). Warm bases employ the fifth-degree fourteen-point symmetric Stroud rule \cite{Stroud:1971} summarized in Table~\ref{tab:stroud14}, $S^2$ spherical shells ($Q \in \{6, 12, 14, 26\}$) normalized by $\sqrt{3}$, or optional $Q_{\rm SCP} \in \{8, 27\}$ product lattices matching unit covariance up to third or fifth order.

All continuum MFM runs use cubic-spline smoothing length closure $h_a^3 \bar{n}_a = 1$, CFL factor $\nu_{\rm CFL} = 0.3$, velocity-crossing slope threshold $\epsilon_v = 0.1$ (disabled under colliding shocks), and harmonic sound-speed thermal conduction coefficient $\kappa_{\rm Noh} = 0.5$ \cite{Noh:1987}. Table~\ref{tab:configs} lists domain extents, mesh parameters, time steps, and particle counts across all relativistic and full-GR testbeds.
