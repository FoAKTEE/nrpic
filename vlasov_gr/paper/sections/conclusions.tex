\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have formulated, proved analytically, and verified numerically a unified general-relativistic hybrid Boltzmann-Vlasov solver in AthenaK bridging collisionless characteristics and collisional hydrodynamics. By coupling symmetry-exact deterministic sampling, fourth-order symplectic geodesic integration, three-level quadratic metric time interpolation, $C^2$ six-node quintic Hermite spatial reconstruction, conservative Cell-BGK relaxation, and self-adjoint harmonic source filtering atop GPU-optimized moving finite volumes, the algorithm achieves both continuum shock fidelity and symplectic phase-space precision inside a single framework.

In collisional regimes ($\sigma/\mu_p > 0$), analytical face-velocity and temperature inversions paired with compact plain-old-data kernel layouts accelerate continuum MFM across curved domains, while Packet Cell-BGK driven by a globally dominating three-component Gamma-mixture Maxwell-J{\"u}ttner sampler enforces local four-momentum conservation to $10^{-14}$ in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ cost per step, beating subcycled Monte Carlo collisions by a factor of fifty at $\sigma/\mu_p = 10^4$.

In collisionless strong-field spacetimes ($\sigma/\mu_p = 0$), two-stage Gauss-Legendre integration cuts eccentric ($e = 0.99$) Kerr pericenter energy drift to $1.0 \times 10^{-13}$ (six orders of magnitude beyond midpoint), tracks dynamical Z4c merger tracer invariants cleanly, and pairs with zero-VRAM-overhead $C^2$ quintic Hermite metric reconstruction ($n_g = 4$, saving $2.38\,\mathrm{GiB}$ relative to B-spline arrays) to eliminate the $\mathcal{O}(\Delta x^3)$ cubic-Hermite spatial floor—lowering $6$-level static-mesh Kerr ($N \le 10^5$, $t=200M$) median Killing drift $311\times$ down to $|\Delta\Egeo/\Egeo|_{\rm med} = 3.38 \times 10^{-10}$. Across $N = 10^4\text{--}10^5$ self-gravitating Oppenheimer-Snyder dust collapse, prolate $e = 0.9$ spindles \cite{Shapiro:1991zza}, Shapiro-Teukolsky star clusters ($y_c \in [0.283, 0.900]$) \cite{1985ApJ...298...58S}, and $8$-level AMR Jeans binary black hole formation ($\Delta x_{\min} = M/64$), symmetry-exact quasi-Monte Carlo pairing holds linear momentum below $2 \times 10^{-14}$ and suppresses spurious remnant spin and vertical kicks, while harmonic metric-weighted refluxing conserves proper-volume ADM integrals to $\le 10^{-15}$. Eliminating matter-freezing Z4c stage bias restores second-order coupled convergence ($15\text{--}23\times$ smaller central-lapse error) and enables high-Courant three-stage \texttt{rk3} ($\nu_{\rm CFL}=0.40\text{--}0.45$), unsplit seven-point conservative filtering with active-block skipping ($\kappa \le 1/12$), and multi-rate \texttt{matter\_stride} subcycling—delivering $3.19\times$ Kerr and $2.24\text{--}2.95\times$ self-gravitating single-GPU speedups toward large-$N$ relativistic kinetic astrophysics.
