\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

We have formulated, proved analytically, and verified numerically a unified general-relativistic hybrid Boltzmann-Vlasov solver in AthenaK bridging collisionless characteristics and collisional hydrodynamics. By coupling symmetry-exact deterministic sampling, fourth-order symplectic geodesic integration, three-level quadratic metric time interpolation, exact conservative Cell-BGK relaxation, and self-adjoint harmonic source filtering atop GPU-optimized moving finite volumes, the algorithm achieves both continuum shock fidelity and symplectic phase-space precision inside a single framework.

In collisional regimes ($\sigma/\mu_p > 0$), analytical face-velocity and temperature inversions paired with compact plain-old-data kernel layouts accelerate continuum MFM dramatically across curved domains, while Packet Cell-BGK driven by a globally dominating three-component Gamma-mixture Maxwell-J{\"u}ttner sampler enforces local four-momentum conservation to $10^{-14}$ in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ cost per step, beating subcycled Monte Carlo collisions by a factor of fifty at $\sigma/\mu_p = 10^4$.

In collisionless strong-field spacetimes ($\sigma/\mu_p = 0$), two-stage Gauss-Legendre symplectic integration improves eccentric ($e = 0.99$) Kerr pericenter energy conservation to $1.0 \times 10^{-13}$ (six orders of magnitude beyond midpoint), matches dynamic Z4c merger tracer invariants cleanly, and reaches $5.8 \times 10^{-16}$ median energy precision in analytic $10^5$-particle Kerr clusters. Across $N = 10^4\text{--}10^5$ self-gravitating Oppenheimer-Snyder, prolate $e = 0.9$ spindle \cite{Shapiro:1991zza}, and Shapiro-Teukolsky star cluster evolutions \cite{1985ApJ...298...58S}, symmetry-exact quasi-Monte Carlo initialization removes sampling noise spikes, prevents delayed horizon formation, holds center-of-mass momentum below $2 \times 10^{-14}$, and suppresses spurious black hole spin twentyfold.

Our $6$-level static-mesh Kerr benchmarks isolate one remaining accuracy bottleneck: once fourth-order time integration eliminates temporal drift, four-point cubic-Hermite spatial metric interpolation imposes an $\mathcal{O}(\Delta x^3)$ gradient floor near $10^{-7}$. Advancing spatial metric reconstruction to $C^4$-smooth fifth-order quintic B-splines and completing full binary-merger cluster tests constitute natural next milestones toward exascale relativistic kinetic astrophysics.
