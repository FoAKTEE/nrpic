\begin{abstract}
Kinetic matter in strong gravitational fields spans two disparate regimes that challenge any single discretization: collisionless phase-space streaming with sharp orbital caustics and collisional relativistic plasma near local thermodynamic equilibrium. Meshless moving-finite-volume moment closures remove sampling noise across finite mean free paths, whereas fourth-order symplectic packet integration preserves geodesic invariants in curved spacetimes without neighbor diffusion. We combine both approaches in a unified GPU-accelerated general-relativistic solver built atop AthenaK. For collisional flows, an asymptotic-preserving finite-volume scheme evolves covariant velocity bases under boosted one-sided Riemann integrals blended continuously into a Synge HLLC fluid solver, alongside a conservative relativistic Cell-BGK packet collision operator driven by a globally dominating Gamma-mixture Maxwell-J{\"u}ttner sampler and exact convex four-momentum projection. This operator enforces energy conservation to rounding precision across four decades of optical depth at constant step cost, achieving a fiftyfold speedup over subcycled Monte Carlo binary pairing in optically thick shocks. For collisionless strong-field transport, deterministic Stroud cubature, spherical Lebedev shells, and symmetry-exact quasi-Monte Carlo sampling pair with a fourth-order two-stage Gauss-Legendre symplectic pusher and three-level quadratic Lagrange metric time interpolation, cutting eccentric Kerr pericenter energy drift by six orders of magnitude, eliminating spurious black hole spin by a factor of twenty in collapsing relativistic clusters, and ensuring proper-volume conservation across adaptive refinement hierarchies via a self-adjoint harmonic flux-conservative filter. Benchmarks from relativistic colliding beams and hundred-thousand-particle Kerr tidal flows through dynamical black hole mergers, prolate spindle collapse, and Shapiro-Teukolsky star clusters establish unified accuracy and strong scaling on a single graphics accelerator.
\end{abstract}
