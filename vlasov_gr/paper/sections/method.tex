\section{Hybrid phase-space algorithm}\label{sec:method}

General-relativistic kinetic transport divides naturally by collisionality. Where $\sigma/\mu_p > 0$, frequent scattering suppresses velocity-space filamentation while driving shocks and thermodynamic relaxation, rewarding either conservative moving-finite-volume (MFM) velocity-basis closures with an asymptotic Riemann solver or an asymptotic-preserving particle Cell-BGK relaxation scheme. Where $\sigma/\mu_p = 0$, multi-stream caustics and orbital phase mixing challenge neighbor-averaged moment closures; evolving deterministic phase-space packets by fourth-order symplectic geodesic integration with low-discrepancy quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC) initialization eliminates neighbor diffusion and preserves Killing invariants near machine precision.

\subsection{Moving-finite-volume basis discretization}\label{sec:method_mfm}

On $N$ mesh-generating Lagrangian elements at positions $\bm{x}_a(t)$, a compact cubic spline kernel $W(\bm{r}, h_a)$ fixes each coordinate smoothing length $h_a$ and effective coordinate volume $V_a = \bar{n}_a^{-1}$ through $h_a^3 \bar{n}_a = 1$, alongside the coordinate kernel moment matrix $\bm{E}_a = \sum_c (\bm{x}_{ca}\otimes\bm{x}_{ca}) W(\bm{x}_{ca}, h_a)$ and exact antisymmetric effective face area covectors \cite{Hopkins:2015mfc,Hopkins:2026lbv}
\begin{multline}\label{eq:mfm_faces}
A_{ab,i} = V_a\!\left(\bm{E}_a^{-1}\bm{x}_{ba}\right)_i W(\bm{x}_{ba},h_a) \\
+ V_b\!\left(\bm{E}_b^{-1}\bm{x}_{ba}\right)_i W(\bm{x}_{ba},h_b) = -A_{ba,i},
\end{multline}
where $\bm{x}_{ba} \equiv \bm{x}_b - \bm{x}_a$ and regularizing $\bm{E}_a$ on boundary vacuum stencils maintains exact antisymmetry $A_{ab,i} = -A_{ba,i}$.

Within element $a$, $f$ is expanded over $N_b$ moving velocity bases $\alpha$ with compact top-hat or Gaussian support, evolving conserved moments $(m_{a,\alpha}, P_{a,\alpha,i}, \Pi_{a,\alpha,ij}) = V_a \int f_{a,\alpha}(1, u_i, u_i u_j)\,\dd^3 u$ that recover rest-mass density $D_{a,\alpha} = m_{a,\alpha}/V_a$, mean momentum $\bar{u}_{a,\alpha,i} = P_{a,\alpha,i}/m_{a,\alpha}$, and symmetric covariance $\Sigma_{a,\alpha,ij} = \Pi_{a,\alpha,ij}/m_{a,\alpha} - \bar{u}_{a,\alpha,i}\bar{u}_{a,\alpha,j}$. Sylvester minor tests verify positive-definiteness during weighted least-squares gradient limiting and realizability repair, reserving full eigendecomposition for nearly degenerate covariances. At each face $ab$, Cholesky orthonormalization maps the reconstructed basis moments into a normal-observer face triad $(\hat{e}_{(1)}^i, \hat{e}_{(2)}^i, \hat{e}_{(3)}^i)$ aligned with $\hat{e}_{(1)}^i \propto \gamma_f^{ij} A_{ab,j}$ and norm $\|A_{ab}\|_f = (\gamma_f^{ij}A_{ab,i}A_{ab,j})^{1/2}$. The one-sided kinetic triad flux of basis $(a,\alpha)$ into cell $b$ is
\begin{multline}\label{eq:onesided_flux}
\bm{F}_{a,\alpha\to b}^{\rm kin} = \alpha_f \|A_{ab}\|_f \!\int_{\frac{u_{(1)}}{W} > \beta_F} \!\!\left(\frac{u_{(1)}}{W} - \beta_F\right) \\
\times \!\begin{pmatrix} 1 & u_{(m)} & u_{(m)}u_{(n)} & W \end{pmatrix}^{\!T} f_{a,\alpha}\,\dd^3 u,
\end{multline}
which collapses via analytical one-dimensional parallel antiderivatives on transverse quadrature nodes $\bm{u}_\perp$ with smooth Gauss-Legendre switchover in cold streams \cite{Hopkins:2026lbv}. Demanding zero net mass transfer across the moving interface determines the normal-observer face velocity $\beta_F \in (-1,1)$ as the unique root of $\Phi(\beta_F) \equiv \sum_\alpha F_{m,a,\alpha\to b}^{\rm kin}(\beta_F) - \sum_\beta F_{m,b,\beta\to a}^{\rm kin}(-\beta_F) = 0$, solved by safeguarded Newton iterations using exact analytical derivatives $\partial\Phi/\partial\beta_F$. Bases pair across faces by minimum dispersion cost in the boosted face rest frame at speed $\beta_F$, while the collisional flux blends smoothly into a relativistic Synge HLLC Riemann flux $\bm{F}_{ab}^{\rm HLLC}$ \cite{Mignone:2005ns} and Noh thermal conduction \cite{Noh:1987}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ap_blend}
\begin{aligned}
\bm{F}_{ab} &= w_c \bm{F}_{ab}^{\rm kin} + (1 - w_c)\bm{F}_{ab}^{\rm HLLC}, \\
w_c &= \exp\!\left(-\frac{\Delta x_{\rm prop} D_f}{\sqrt{\gamma_f}}\frac{\sigma}{\mu_p}\right),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
yielding second-order accuracy from free streaming ($w_c = 1$) to dense relativistic hydrodynamics ($w_c \to 0$).

\subsection{Symplectic integration and metric time interpolation}\label{sec:method_gl4}

Along the normal-observer characteristic equations for canonical packet state $\bm{z} = (x^i, u_i)^T$, standard second-order implicit midpoint integration accumulates secular phase error along eccentric strong-field orbits. To push curved-space precision to fourth order, each packet evolves via the two-stage Gauss-Legendre implicit Runge-Kutta scheme over step $[t^n, t^n + \Delta t_n]$:
\begin{align}\label{eq:gl4_tableau}
\bm{Z}_s &= \bm{z}^n + \Delta t_n \sum_{r=1}^{2} a_{sr}\,\bm{f}(t^n + c_r \Delta t_n, \bm{Z}_r), \nonumber\\
\bm{z}^{n+1} &= \bm{z}^n + \Delta t_n \sum_{s=1}^{2} b_s\,\bm{f}(t^n + c_s \Delta t_n, \bm{Z}_s),
\end{align}
with collocation nodes $c_{1,2} = \frac{1}{2} \mp \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$, quadrature weights $b_1 = b_2 = \frac{1}{2}$, and Runge-Kutta matrix
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl4_A}
A = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{4} & \frac{1}{4} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} \\ \frac{1}{4} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} & \frac{1}{4} \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Because $b_r a_{rs} + b_s a_{sr} - b_r b_s = 0$ holds identically for $r,s \in \{1,2\}$, this pusher is canonical (symplectic), time-reversible, and $A$-stable, conserving $\dd\bm{x}\wedge\dd\bm{u}$ exactly and modified Hamiltonians to $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t_n^4)$. Both stage residuals converge cleanly in fixed-point iteration near machine precision.

In dynamical Z4c spacetimes, stage metrics $\bm{g}_{\rm ADM}(t^n + c_s\Delta t_n, \bm{Z}_s)$ are evaluated across three stored metric time slices $(t^{n-1}, t^n, t^{n+1})$ without extra Z4c right-hand-side calls by retaining the previous slice via a zero-copy buffer swap. With step ratio $r = \Delta t_n / \Delta t_{n-1}$, quadratic Lagrange time interpolation at stage fraction $c \in (0, 1)$ uses
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lagrange3}
\begin{aligned}
w_{-1}(c) &= \frac{r^2 c(c-1)}{1+r}, \qquad w_0(c) = (1-c)(1+rc), \\
w_1(c) &= \frac{c(1+rc)}{1+r},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
reducing temporal metric truncation error from $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^2)$ under linear two-level blending to $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^3)$, with automatic linear fallback immediately following startup or adaptive regridding. Spatial mesh metric fields and derivatives use four-point cubic Hermite interpolation on the six-level static or adaptive refinement hierarchy \cite{Wu:2026nrpic}.

\subsection{Cubature packets and quasi-Monte Carlo sampling}\label{sec:method_scp}

When $\sigma/\mu_p \le 0$, warm MFM velocity bases expand once at initialization into deterministic symplectic cubature packets \cite{Stroud:1971}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scp_map}
\bm{u}_{a,\alpha,q} = \bar{\bm{u}}_{a,\alpha} + \bm{L}_{a,\alpha}\bm{R}_{a,\alpha}\bm{\xi}_q, \qquad m_{a,\alpha,q} = m_{a,\alpha} w_q,
\end{equation}
where $\bm{L}_{a,\alpha}\bm{L}_{a,\alpha}^T = \Sigma_{a,\alpha}$ is the Cholesky factor and $\bm{R}_{a,\alpha} \in \mathrm{SO}(3)$ is drawn from the base-$2,3,5$ Halton quaternion sequence \cite{Halton:1960}. For isotropic Maxwellians and shells on $S^2$, rotational cubature sets $(\hat{\bm{n}}_q, w_q)$ of size $Q \in \{6, 12, 14, 26\}$ (octahedral, icosahedral, and Lebedev rules exact up to spherical-harmonic degree $\ell = 7$) satisfy $\sum_q w_q (\sqrt{3}\hat{\bm{n}}_q)(\sqrt{3}\hat{\bm{n}}_q)^T = \bm{I}_3$, matching arbitrary local pressure tensors to machine precision.

To suppress Poisson fluctuations in global self-gravitating initial data at finite packet budget $N = N_s Q$, spatial coordinates are drawn from radially rank-stratified five-dimensional scrambled Sobol sequences combined with symmetry-exact velocity pairings:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textit{Antipodal four-tuple pairing} ($Q = 4$): pairing $\{(\pm\bm{x}, \pm\bm{u})\}$ at each Sobol radial sample zeroes the center of mass $\bm{X}_{\rm cm}$, total linear momentum $\bm{P}$, angular momentum $\bm{J}$, and local normal-observer momentum density $S_i$ algebraically to rounding precision while allocating $N_s = N/4$ spatial sites.
\item \textit{Rotational $S^2$ Lebedev shells} ($Q = 14$): centering $14$-point Lebedev shells at $N_s = N/14$ sites reduces local stress-tensor anisotropy to machine precision ($\sim 10^{-16}$), proving optimal for large budgets ($N \ge 10^5$) where $N_s$ remains dense across adaptive mesh cells.
\item \textit{Discrete reflection groups}: spherical dust models employ $24$-fold $O_h$ cubic symmetry ($Q_{xx}=Q_{yy}=Q_{zz}$), while axisymmetric prolate spheroids employ $16$-fold $(x \leftrightarrow y)$ octant reflections ($Q_{xx}=Q_{yy}$, zero off-diagonal quadrupole moments).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Asymptotic-preserving conservative Cell-BGK operator}\label{sec:method_bgk}

Pure Monte Carlo binary pairing scales linearly with collision optical depth at high collisionality, whereas continuum MFM introduces gradient diffusion across collisionless streams. Equipping geodesic packets with an asymptotic-preserving conservative Cell-BGK relaxation operator handles both limits in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ work per step as $\sigma/\mu_p \to \infty$.

Packets are bucketed directly into computational subcells via global face tables independent of mesh-block decomposition. In each subcell $c$ containing rest mass $M_c = \sum_{p\in c} m_p$, normal-observer energy $E_c$, and momentum $\bm{P}_c$ measured in the local orthonormal ADM triad, invariant mass $M_{\rm inv} = \sqrt{E_c^2 - |\bm{P}_c|^2} > M_c$ determines comoving mean Lorentz factor $\bar{W}_{\rm com} = M_{\rm inv}/M_c$ and temperature $\theta$ by analytical Newton inversion of the Synge enthalpy in Eq.~(\ref{eq:synge_eos}). Each packet enters local thermal relaxation with exponential probability $p_{\rm coll} = 1 - e^{-\tau_{\rm coll}}$, where $\tau_{\rm coll} = M_c (\sigma/\mu_p) v_{\rm rel}\alpha\Delta t / (V_c U_0^2)$ and $v_{\rm rel}$ denotes the M{\o}ller relative speed.

Post-collision comoving momenta $\bm{q}_p$ draw exact kinetic energies $x = W - 1 \ge 0$ from the isotropic Maxwell-J{\"u}ttner marginal $f(x) \propto x^{1/2}(1+x)\sqrt{2+x}\,e^{-x/\theta}$ using a global three-component Gamma-mixture majorant valid for all $\theta > 0$:
\begin{multline}\label{eq:mj_majorant}
h(x) = \left(\sqrt{2}\,x^{1/2} + 2\,x^{3/2} + x^2\right)e^{-x/\theta} \\
\ge x^{1/2}(1+x)\sqrt{2+x}\,e^{-x/\theta},
\end{multline}
sampled directly from $\mathrm{Gamma}(3/2,\theta)$, $\mathrm{Gamma}(5/2,\theta)$, and $\mathrm{Gamma}(3,\theta)$ in proportions $1 : \frac{3}{\sqrt{2}}\theta : \sqrt{\frac{8}{\pi}}\theta^{3/2}$ via deterministic Philox random numbers. Writing $t = \sqrt{x} \ge 0$ proves global domination algebraically:
\begin{multline}\label{eq:mj_proof}
\left(\sqrt{2} + 2t^2 + t^3\right)^2 - (1 + t^2)^2(2 + t^2) \\
= (4\sqrt{2} - 5)t^2 + 2\sqrt{2}\,t^3 + 4t^5 \ge 0,
\end{multline}
since $4\sqrt{2} - 5 > 0.65 > 0$, ensuring acceptance efficiency exceeding sixty-five percent across all nonrelativistic and ultrarelativistic temperatures.

Finite-sample fluctuation in post-draw comoving four-momentum $(E_t, \bm{P}_t)$ is removed by an exact three-step cell projection for arbitrary unequal packet weights $m_p$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Boost all post-draw momenta into the sample center-of-momentum frame ($\bm{P}_{\rm com} = \sum_p m_p \bm{q}_p^*$) and subtract roundoff drift $\bm{q}_p^* \leftarrow \bm{q}_p^* - \bm{P}_{\rm com}/M_c$.
\item Rescale $\bm{q}_p^* \leftarrow s\,\bm{q}_p^*$ using the unique positive root $s > 0$ of the strictly increasing convex function
\begin{equation}\label{eq:proj_convex}
F(s) = \sum_{p\in c} m_p \sqrt{1 + s^2 |\bm{q}_p^*|^2} = M_{\rm inv},
\end{equation}
solved to machine precision via safeguarded Newton-bisection from $F(0) = M_c < M_{\rm inv}$.
\item Boost $s\bm{q}_p^*$ forward by $U_{\rm cm}^{\hat{\mu}} = (E_c, \bm{P}_c)/M_{\rm inv}$, enforcing exact cell energy and three-momentum conservation down to rounding error.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Self-adjoint metric-weighted conservative source filter}\label{sec:method_filter}

Packets deposit normal-observer moments onto the adaptive grid via cloud-in-cell or quadratic B-spline clouds \cite{Wu:2026nrpic}. Plain binomial filtering $(w_1, 1-2w_1, w_1)^3$ applied directly to physical fields $q = \{E, S_i, S_{ij}\}$ violates the proper-volume ADM integral $\int q\sqrt{\gamma}\,\dd^3 x$ wherever $\nabla\sqrt{\gamma} \ne 0$. Conversely, filtering densitized fields $\sqrt{\gamma}q$ on one pass preserves the integral on uniform grids but distorts constant curved states ($q = \text{const} \not\mapsto \text{const}$) and leaks energy across adaptive refinement boundaries without flux matching.

Both errors vanish under a self-adjoint metric-weighted flux-conservative filter sweeping coordinate directions $d \in \{1, 2, 3\}$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mcons_filter}
\begin{aligned}
q_c^{\rm new} &= q_c - \frac{F_{c+1/2} - F_{c-1/2}}{\sqrt{\gamma}_c \Delta V_c}, \\
F_{c+1/2} &= -w_1 \sigma_{c+1/2} \Delta V_{c+1/2}\,(q_{c+1} - q_c),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with harmonic face metric conductance
\begin{equation}\label{eq:harmonic_cond}
\sigma_{c+1/2} = \frac{2\sqrt{\gamma}_c \sqrt{\gamma}_{c+1}}{\sqrt{\gamma}_c + \sqrt{\gamma}_{c+1}},
\end{equation}
and face flux matching (refluxing) accumulated across coarse-fine adaptive boundaries. By direct summation, this operator satisfies four algebraic guarantees simultaneously on any smooth or rough adaptive metric: constant physical fields are exact fixed points ($q_c \equiv q_0 \Rightarrow F_{c+1/2} \equiv 0$); proper-volume integrals $\sum_c \sqrt{\gamma}_c q_c \Delta V_c$ are conserved to machine precision across refinement jumps; the discrete filter is symmetric negative semi-definite (self-adjoint and $L^2(\sqrt{\gamma}\,\dd V)$-contractive) for $w_1 \le 1/4$; and pointwise positivity $q^{\rm new} \ge 0$ holds unconditionally when $w_1 \le 1/4$ because diagonal weights remain nonnegative since $\sigma_{c\pm 1/2} \le 2\sqrt{\gamma}_c$.

To eliminate redundant inter-block synchronization, we also formulate an unsplit seven-point harmonic filter advancing all three axes in a single pass with isotropic face conductance $\sigma_{d\pm}$ and coupling parameter $\kappa$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unsplit_mcons}
\begin{aligned}
q_c^{\rm new} = q_c + \frac{\kappa}{\sqrt{\gamma}_c \Delta V_c}\sum_{d=1}^{3}
\Big[&\sigma_{d+}\Delta V_{d+}(q_{c+\hat{e}_d} - q_c) \\
&- \sigma_{d-}\Delta V_{d-}(q_c - q_{c-\hat{e}_d})\Big],
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
which cuts ghost-cell and flux-boundary exchanges threefold and skips empty vacuum blocks using an active-block mask and cached metric determinant. Unconditional positivity holds for $\kappa \le 1/12$ (since $\sigma_{d\pm} \le 2\sqrt{\gamma}_c$ bounds the diagonal weight above $1 - 12\kappa \ge 0$), preserving exact constant states ($0.00$), self-adjointness ($1.43 \times 10^{-16}$), rotational isotropy ($2.22 \times 10^{-16}$), and two-level adaptive conservation ($5.11 \times 10^{-16}$) as verified in Fig.~\ref{fig:iter3_mcons}. Its dispersion factor $1 - 4\kappa\sum_d \sin^2(k_d\Delta x/2)$ illustrates an algorithmic trade-off: at matched second moment ($\kappa = 1/6$ or $n_{\rm f}=2\text{--}3$ passes at $\kappa = 1/12$), unsplit filtering yields $2\times\text{--}3.2\times$ higher GPU throughput on static-metric and high-PPC ($10^5$) runs, whereas directional $w_1 = 1/4$ sweeps completely annihilate axis-aligned Nyquist modes $(\pi,0,0)$ in a single pass where $\kappa = 1/12$ retains factor $2/3$.

\subsection{Quintic metric reconstruction and multi-rate subcycling}\label{sec:method_quintic_multirate}

High-order symplectic particle integration exposes spatial metric interpolation error once temporal drift falls below $\sim 10^{-12}$. Replacing four-point cubic Hermite interpolation with a zero-overhead $C^2$ six-node quintic Hermite stencil (within the standard four ghost-cell width $n_g = 4$) eliminates the $\mathcal{O}(\Delta x^3)$ gradient discontinuity without allocating auxiliary B-spline coefficient arrays, saving $2.38\,\mathrm{GiB}$ of device memory on $6$-level Kerr grids while tightening static-mesh orbit errors by two to three orders of magnitude.

For full $3+1$ self-gravitating evolution, throughput is governed equally by Z4c right-hand-side evaluations and conservative filter sweeps. Pairing continuous first-order proper-time integration (Scheme~A) with the three-stage strong-stability-preserving Z4c integrator (\texttt{rk3}) raises the stable CFL limit from $\nu_{\rm CFL} = 0.25$ (\texttt{rk4}) to $\nu_{\rm CFL} = 0.40\text{--}0.45$, while multi-rate kinetic subcycling advances particles once per macro-step of stride $s_{\rm m}$ using three-level quadratic metric reconstruction across substeps. Coupled linear-wave kernel tests confirm second-order accuracy ($p = 1.98$ under multi-rate \texttt{rk3} and \texttt{rk4}, vs $p = 0.99$ under frozen metrics) while cutting coupled GPU time to solution by $2.2\times\text{--}2.95\times$.

\subsection{Task-list modularization and GPU kernel layout}\label{sec:method_gpu}

Both kinetic branches execute within an explicit modular task list inside AthenaK: step preparation, three-level metric synchronization, fourth-order geodesic or continuum finite-volume advance, Cell-BGK relaxation, lapse excision, metric-weighted deposit filtering with adaptive boundary refluxing, and Z4c source injection. Boundary-only reflux storage shrinks GPU filter memory by $6{,}064\,\mathrm{MiB}$ on adaptive collapse grids, while active-basis template specialization ($N_b \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$), mean-$\bar{h}$ spatial hash binning with symmetric gather-scatter neighbor lists, and compact plain-old-data kernel pointer packing eliminate register spills, constant-memory launch stalls, and quadratic neighbor scaling under strong shear without altering physical equations.
