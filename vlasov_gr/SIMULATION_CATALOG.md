# Simulation Catalog and Curated PRX Paper Subset

This external catalog tracks every simulation campaign executed for the general-relativistic kinetic project (archived on scratch and in `vlasov_gr/paper/data/*.npz`), records the curation rationale selecting the cleanest, non-redundant subset for the Physical Review X (PRX) manuscript, and maps the resulting **15 curated figures** (3 methodology schematics + 12 simulation figures) to their scripts, datasets, and manuscript sections.

> **Curation criteria (PRX methodology + Sec. 7.6):**
> 1. Include every distinct physical regime and numerical building block once cleanly, eliminating duplicate intermediate snapshots (`kerr_single_particle`, `kerr_cluster_orbits`, separate `st_*` / `jeans_*` splits, redundant VRAM/filter sub-checks).
> 2. Exclude all internal development, regression, parity, and compiler/launch debugging runs (`smoke_geo`, `stage3_parity*`, `phase1/2_verify`, `pbgk_diag`, `iter4_step*`, `iter5_diag_iso*`, `bh_massive_sanity`, `stage4a_ablation`) per Rule 7.6.
> 3. Express all performance comparisons in figures as dimensionless ratios relative to baseline configurations; keep explicit parameter/timing tables only in Appendix~\ref{app:params} and Supplemental Material.
