## 1. Stage 1 Panel (c) & (d): Relativistic Hydrodynamic Shock Limit

### Panel (d) — Central Blue Line Dip ($\sigma/\mu = 500$, $u_0 = \pm 1$)
Four coupled issues degraded the high-collisionality Taub shock limit in [vlasov_gr.py](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/python/nrpic/vlasov_gr.py) and [nrpic_vlasov_kernels.hpp](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/athenak/src/particles/nrpic_vlasov_kernels.hpp):

1. **Pressureless vs. Exact Valencia Synge C2P**: `BuildHllcCellFluid` and the HLLC star-state tensor evaluation previously inverted $(D, E, \mathbf{P})$ using `v = P / E` and `Minv = sqrt(E^2 - P^2)`. For a relativistic gas with pressure $p = \rho_0 \theta > 0$, the exact Eulerian conserved identity in an orthonormal triad is $E = D h U^0 - p$, $\mathbf{P} = D h U^0 \mathbf{v} \neq E\mathbf{v}$. We implemented `ValenciaC2PSynge` / `valencia_c2p_synge`, which solves
   $$E(\theta) = D h_{\rm Synge}(\theta) U^0(\theta) - \frac{D}{U^0(\theta)}\,\theta, \qquad U^0(\theta) = \sqrt{1 + \frac{|\mathbf{P}|^2}{\left(D h_{\rm Synge}(\theta)\right)^2}}$$
   to machine precision (`< 1e-13`) via Brent root-finding, recovering $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{P}/(D h U^0)$ and $p = (D/U^0)\theta$.
2. **Asymmetric Contact Flux Branch**: When `use_contact_beta_F = true`, $\beta_{\rm use} = S_\star$, so testing $\beta_{\rm use} \le S_\star$ unconditionally selected the left star state $L_\star$. Across symmetric colliding shocks this broke left-right parity (`D31 = 3.4539` vs `D32 = 3.3885`). We now set exact contact fluxes ($F^{(D)}_{\rm ALE} = 0$, $F^{(P)}_{\rm ALE} = (p_\star, 0, 0)$, $F^{(E)}_{\rm ALE} = p_\star S_\star$) and symmetrize $\mathbf{F}^{(\Pi)}_{\rm ALE} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{F}^{(\Pi)}_{L_\star} + \mathbf{F}^{(\Pi)}_{R_\star})$.
3. **Shock Slope Limiter Bypass**: The Hopkins velocity-crossing threshold `eps_v = 0.1` skipped Barth-Jespersen limiting on adjacent cells during shock formation, creating post-shock even-odd ringing. Setting `lim_eps_v = 0.0` whenever `coll_sigma > 0` enforces monotone TVD limiting on fluid shocks without altering collisionless stream crossing.
4. **Intrinsic MFM Noh Wall Heating**: Even with exact C2P, a strictly Lagrangian ($F^{(D)} = 0$) MFM shock from $M = \infty$ cold streams traps excess specific thermal energy in the central interface cells at $t = 0\text{--}0.03$. At uniform downstream pressure $p_2$, constant-mass cells cannot shed that thermal excess, locking in a $\sim 15\%$ central density dip (`D_mid = 3.501` even at `noh_kappa = 0.0`). We added a conservative, shock-localized harmonic-$c_s$ Noh/Price thermal conduction flux in `NohThermalConductFlux` / `noh_thermal_conduct_flux`:
   $$F^{(E)}_{\rm cond} = \kappa_{\rm th}\,\frac{D_L + D_R}{2}\, v_{\rm sig}\, (w_{m,L} - w_{m,R}), \qquad v_{\rm sig} = \min\!\left(\frac{2 c_{s,L} c_{s,R}}{c_{s,L} + c_{s,R} + \epsilon},\; \sqrt{\frac{|p_L - p_R|}{\bar{\rho}_0}} + \max(v_{x,L} - v_{x,R}, 0)\right)$$
   weighted by $(1 - w_c)$ inside the collisional HLLC channel (`noh_kappa = 0.5`). By construction, $F^{(E)}_{\rm cond}$ is antisymmetric (exact energy conservation: `dE = 7.85e-16`), vanishes identically in collisionless regimes ($1 - w_c = 0$), vanishes ahead of cold shocks ($c_s = 0 \implies v_{\rm sig} = 0$), and vanishes across pressure- and velocity-balanced contacts ($p_L = p_R, v_L = v_R \implies v_{\rm sig} = 0$). On Stage 1 panel (d), `D_mid` improves from `3.4212` ($-17.39\%$) to **`4.1142` ($-0.66\%$)** vs exact $D_2 = 4.1414$.

## 2. Stage 4a Panel (b): Suite 1D $\sigma/\mu = 15$ Central Dispersion

- **Root Cause**: In both `ComputeEdgeFluxes` ([nrpic_vlasov.cpp](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/athenak/src/particles/nrpic_vlasov.cpp)) and `_compute_1d_rhs` ([vlasov_gr.py](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/python/nrpic/vlasov_gr.py)), `RelHllcFluxTetrad` was invoked with `use_contact_beta_F = (wc < 0.5)`. In the intermediate regime `s1d_sig15` ($\sigma/\mu = 15$), central cells satisfy $\Delta x D (\sigma/\mu) \approx 0.37 \implies w_c = e^{-0.37} \approx 0.69 \ge 0.5$. Evaluating the $(1 - w_c)$ HLLC mass flux at the kinetic face velocity $\beta_F^{\rm kin} \neq S_\star$ yielded nonzero $F^{(D)}_{\rm ALE} = D_\star(S_\star - \beta_F^{\rm kin})$ that systematically transported mass from hotter/lower-$D$ cells into cooler/higher-$D$ cells, driving a secular clumping instability (`m/m0 in [0.0465, 2.2204]`, central $D \in [0.209, 8.265]$).
- **Fix**: Unconditionally pass `use_contact_beta_F = true` whenever the HLLC channel is active (`wc < 1.0 - 1.0e-14`), ensuring both kinetic ($F^{(m)}_{\rm kin}(\beta_F^{\rm kin}) = 0$) and HLLC ($F^{(D)}_{\rm HLLC}(S_\star) = 0$) fluxes strictly conserve per-element rest mass for all $\sigma/\mu \in [0, \infty)$.
- **Verification**: Re-running Suite 1D on the B200 GPU eliminates element mass drift to machine precision (`m/m0 in [1.000000, 1.000000]`, `std = 7.68e-13`) and collapses central $D$ scatter by $23.3\times$ (`mean = 3.6716`, `std = 0.0522`, range `[3.5590, 3.7979]`), smoothly bridging $\sigma/\mu = 2$ and $\sigma/\mu = 10^4$ (`D_cent = 4.0671` vs exact `4.1006`, $-0.82\%$).

## 3. Stage 4a Panel (d): Suite 2 Kerr $a = 0.9 M$ Killing Invariant Drift

- **Root Causes**:
  1. **Pre-horizon drift ($t \le 20 M$, `~3.4e-4` at $N=3112$)**: `ComputeGradients` previously zeroed all three gradient fields (`grad_D, grad_u, grad_S`) whenever `is_bnd(a) == 1` (13–30% of elements on the cloud surface), degrading smooth velocity-shear reconstruction to first order; additionally `NrpicVlasovPush` applied a single un-subcycled bracketed geodesic half-kick around `n_sub` MFM substeps.
  2. **Post-$20 M$ blowup (`5.79e-3` at $N=3112$, `3.92e-2` at $N=552$)**: The original Suite 2 orbital initialization (`u_bl[:,0] = -0.10`, `u_bl[:,2] = 0.92 * Lz_circ`) with `alpha_exc = 0.0` plunged past the Kerr event horizon ($r_+ = 1.436 M$) down to $r_{\rm BL} \approx 0.65 M$ inside the Cauchy horizon where Cartesian Kerr-Schild gradients diverge near the ring singularity (where lapse stays $\alpha > 0.5$, bypassing `alpha_exc`).
- **Fixes Applied**:
  1. In `ComputeGradients` ([nrpic_vlasov.cpp](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/athenak/src/particles/nrpic_vlasov.cpp)): zero only `grad_D` (vacuum density edge) while retaining Barth-Jespersen / SPD-limited `grad_u` and `grad_S` for boundary elements with `cnt >= 4`.
  2. In `Particles::NrpicVlasovPush` ([nrpic_vlasov.cpp](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/athenak/src/particles/nrpic_vlasov.cpp)): on static or analytic spacetimes, subcycle symmetric Strang kick-drift-kick (`K(dt_sub/2) -> MFM(dt_sub) -> K(dt_sub/2)`) inside every CFL substep (leaving the dynamic Z4c Suite 3 path untouched).
  3. In `Particles::NrpicVlasovExcise` ([nrpic_vlasov.hpp](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/athenak/src/particles/nrpic_vlasov.hpp), [nrpic_vlasov.cpp](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/athenak/src/particles/nrpic_vlasov.cpp)): added runtime option `vlasov_kerr_r_exc` (default `0.0`) excising elements below $r_{\rm BL} < \text{vlasov\_kerr\_r\_exc} \cdot r_+$ in analytic Kerr metrics.
  4. In [run_stage4a_suites123.py](file:///home/schmidt/ssci-haiyangw/nrpic/vlasov_gr/notes/scripts/run_stage4a_suites123.py): updated Suite 2 (matching both GR-LBV and $10^4$ NRPIC) to a strong-field prograde grazing orbit (`u_bl[:,0] = -0.03`, `1.02 * Lz`, `vlasov_kerr_r_exc = 0.98`) that sweeps $\sim 113^\circ$ down to $r_{\rm BL,min} = 1.702 M > r_+$ inside the ergosphere with zero horizon absorption (`dM = 1.11e-16`), and updated panel (d) legend labels to use exact array counts (`N=552`, `N=3112`).
- **Verification**:
  - **Original plunging IC ($t \le 20 M$, before horizon entry)**: fixed AthenaK reduces `max|dE/E0|` at $N=3112$ from `~3.4e-4` to **`6.22e-5`** (`max|dLz/Lz0| = 4.52e-5`); with `vlasov_kerr_r_exc = 1.0`, elements reaching $r_+$ are cleanly excised (`m_fin = 0.9528` at $N=3112$, zero NaNs).
  - **Strong-field grazing Suite 2 ($t \in [0, 30]\,M$, zero excision)**:
