WHAT IT IS FOR
With outflow faces the stock code (a) fills the ghost cells by linear extrapolation and
(b) applies a Sommerfeld condition that assumes a 1/r fall-off. (a) makes a layer of
Hamiltonian residual 8 x 1.1 m / r^3 in the boundary cells from the first step, which
does not fall with the cell size and dominates whole-mesh norms; (b) drives every field
that is stationary but not 1/r (Bowen-York and Painleve-Gullstrand curvature) and makes
the momentum-constraint layer; both send an error inward at the speed of light.

NEW OPTIONS (block <z4c>)
Example input with all of them: problems/tests/z4c_faces.athinput (vacuum puncture,
faces at 16 M; 30 core-minutes to t = 12, or time/nlim=8 for 80 s):
    athena_cpu -i problems/tests/z4c_faces.athinput -d RUN
The tested combination ("the full set") is
    extrap_order = 4, ghost_falloff = true, sbc_variant = nonwave, sbc_speed = metric

1. ghost_falloff = true | false (default false); ghost_power_metric, ghost_power_curv
   (default 1). Ghost cells at outflow, diode and vacuum faces extrapolate
   (f - f_inf) r^q instead of f (f_inf = 1 for chi, gt_xx, gt_yy, gt_zz, alpha; 0
   otherwise; r from the origin of the mesh) with the weights of the stock fill. Use it
   with extrap_order = 4 (an existing option; needs nghost >= 4).
     athena_cpu -i problems/tests/z4c_faces.athinput -d RUN z4c/sbc_variant=sommerfeld z4c/sbc_speed=unit
   Tested: boundary-cell |H| of a puncture after one step 7.9e-6 against 1.9e-3 (c0);
   face-layer mean |H| 10 to 14 times lower at order 2 alone (c2); a refined region that
   touches a face, also the coarse array before prolongation (t1b).
2. sbc_variant = nonwave | sommerfeld (default sommerfeld); sbc_power_khat, sbc_power_a,
   sbc_power_gam (default 3); sbc_v_khat (default sqrt 2). In the outermost active
   cells d_t f = R[f] + (d_t f - R[f])_p (r_p / r)^q for Khat, Gamma^i, A_ij, with R the
   Sommerfeld operator and p the cell one step inward: exact for a stationary r^-(q-1).
     athena_cpu -i problems/tests/z4c_faces.athinput -d RUN problem/comp0_p1=0.3 problem/comp0_corr=approx
   Tested: ball, Bowen-York puncture (momentum constraint in the face layer 13 times
   lower, c2, c5). Only together with option 1 and order 4: on linear stock ghosts it
   doubles the layer of a boosted puncture (c0b).
3. sbc_speed = unit | lapse | metric (default unit). Speeds of R[f]: 1 (sbc_v_khat for
   Khat); alpha and sqrt(f alpha) with shift advection; the coordinate speeds
   alpha sqrt(chi / gt_ss) with shift advection.
     athena_cpu -i problems/tests/z4c_faces.athinput -d RUN z4c/sbc_speed=lapse
   Tested: metric in every long run. Against unit, in the ball: face-layer difference
   to the reference 26 per cent lower at t = 8, mean |H| 1.5 times lower at t = 16; in
   the vacuum puncture: shell mean |H| 3 per cent higher at t = 12. lapse in the ball
   only (within 1 per cent of metric at t = 8, stable to t = 16).
4. sbc_theta = sommerfeld | nonwave | cpbc (default sommerfeld); sbc_power_theta
   (default 3). Theta in the boundary cells: as option 2, or d_t Theta = -alpha s^k
   d_k Theta + beta^k d_k Theta (arXiv:1212.2901).
     athena_cpu -i problems/tests/z4c_faces.athinput -d RUN z4c/sbc_theta=cpbc
   Tested in the ball only: both are stable to t = 16 and gain nothing (face-layer mean
   |Theta| 3 to 21 and 17 to 39 per cent higher). Leave it at sommerfeld.
The expanding ball of the tests (initial data by problems/ic/make_ball.py):
    python3 problems/ic/make_ball.py --M 0.5 --Rb 6 --edge 1.5 --h 0.5 --tracers "" --sample 0 --out ID --prefix ball
    athena_cpu -i tracks/core/analysis/faces/inputs/ball_ext.athinput -d RUN \
       z4c/extrap_order=4 z4c/ghost_falloff=true z4c/sbc_variant=nonwave z4c/sbc_speed=metric \
       particles/particle_file=ID/ball.nrp problem/comp0_file=ID/ball.tab

  (untouched); reflect, inflow or user faces beside outflow faces.
REMOVED
  sbc_damp_gam, sbc_damp_theta (commit cb50e22): damping of the Gamma constraint in the
  boundary cells was unstable in the ball (non-finite at t = 15), damping of Theta gained
  nothing. A non-zero value of either stops the run at start.
