New options (problem generator nrpic_cf, block <problem>, a component of type table,
format 1: an equilibrium cluster model)
  comp<c>_boost_file     boost table of the cluster (format bst1, docs/FORMATS.md);
                         without it nothing below is read
  comp<c>_p1, _p2, _p3   momentum P of the cluster: sum m u_i of its particles; default 0
                         (zero: the cluster at rest, nothing of the table is used)
  comp<c>_corr           none (default) or table: second-order correction of psi
  comp<c>_corr_file      the correction table (format mp1) written by the generator
  comp<c>_corr_axis1..3  axis of the correction; default from the file, else P
  comp<c>_boost_shift    true: start with the shift of coordinates that move with the
                         cluster; default false (it decays within 1/shift_eta: see below)
The generator adds K_ij = psi^-2 Abar_ij with the curvature of the static cluster on the
maximal slice of its boosted spacetime (radial moments Q, I4 of its momentum density
from the boost table), which solves the momentum constraint for particles boosted by the
generator's rule; it adds linearly to everything else. With the correction the
Hamiltonian constraint holds to second order in the speed for an isolated cluster.
AthenaK takes on the command line only parameters the input file has: list them in the
input (value 0 / none / false) if a variant switches them on. Several clusters: use
lapse = isotropic.

SET THE SHIFT DAMPING FOR A MOVING CLUSTER: z4c/shift_eta = 0.03/M to 0.1/M (M the mass of
the cluster). With 2/M (the value of the inputs for a cluster at rest) the cluster moves
at 0.7 v in coordinates and its summed momentum sum m u_x drifts by 12 per cent in 50 M:
coordinates, not physics (tests TC, D, TF below). The two card examples carry shift_eta =
0.1 (the runs v0.3_e01 and orbit_e01 of test TF); the first tests ran them with 2.0.

Generator
  python3 generator/problems/ic/make_ic.py boosted \
      --cluster yc,mass,x,y,z,vx,vy,vz [--cluster ...] \
      [--cluster-p yc,mass,x,y,z,px,py,pz ...] [--puncture m,x,y,z[,px,py,pz] ...] \
      --N <per cluster> --seed 1 --prefix <stem> --out <rundir> [--boost-shift]
writes <stem>.nrp (species = cluster), <stem>_c<k>.tab, .bst, _corr.tab and <stem>.json;
put athenak.problem of the JSON into the input (comp<c>_p1..3 there are the sums over
the sample). mass = ADM mass of the isolated cluster, yc = model of st_cluster.STModel
(0.819: the stable cluster of the first paper). Tables written before 2026-11-19 16:40Z
(commit baa6f99e) carry a defect of the correction at the surface: write them again.

What is tested (run ids in the journal and in section 6 of the interface)
  - Python reference: momentum of the data = M V to 1e-14 (maximal slice; the plain
    boost would give 0.947 M V); curvature equal to the geometry of the boosted static
    solution to 4e-11; divergence = 8 pi Stilde to 6e-11; boosted particles carry the
    momentum density (10^5 particles: 1.00004 +- 0.0022); the correction adds M V^2/2
    to the ADM mass within 8e-6 for y_c = 0.6 to 0.95
  - one cluster at t = 0, v = 0.1 and 0.3, 32/64/128 cells: momentum-constraint norm with
    the boost table 0.070, 0.034, 0.015 of the uncorrected one (first order in the cell
    size: the edge of the cluster); the curvature term equals the continuum to 4e-6; the
    second-order Hamiltonian residual equals the continuum value (0.97-0.998) and the
    correction leaves 0.046, 0.022, 0.010 of it (v = 0.1) and 0.054, 0.034, 0.026
    (v = 0.3; the terms of order V^4 are 0.023); code fields equal the reference to 6e-8
  - 10^4 equal-mass particles: the smooth part of the momentum violation is removed
    (projection 0.995 -> -0.004); the remaining norm is sampling noise
  - two clusters at t = 0 (x = -20, +20): Hamiltonian residual near a cluster 1.027 times
    that of the isolated one; momentum-constraint ratio 0.035
  - 50 M on a card, dx = M/2, 10^4 particles, v = 0.1 and 0.3: no particle lost; radii of
    10 to 90 per cent of the rest mass within 2.6 and 4.9 per cent of their start (1.2
    per cent of the cluster at rest with shift_eta = 0.1); central lapse 0.0020 and
    0.017-0.019 below the cluster at rest, settled within 10 M; constraint norms at most
    1.04 and 1.17 times those of the cluster at rest
  - the gauge (v = 0.3): shift_eta = 2, 0.3, 0.1, 0.03 give 0.72, 0.84, 0.90, 0.95 v over
    40-50 M and a drift of sum m u_x of -11.8, -5.1, -3.3, -2.5 per cent at 50 M; lapse,
    profile and constraint norms do not depend on it; a cluster at rest is unchanged;
    four times the particles change nothing (not noise). From the restart files: the
    coordinate lines crowd along the motion (g~_xx = 0.886 at the particles), and
    sum m u_x/gamma_xx^(1/2) drifts by 3.4 per cent where sum m u_x drifts by 11.8
  - binary (separation 30 M, first post-Newtonian momenta), shift_eta = 0.1, 125 M:
    separation 29.99 -> 29.62, angular velocity over 40-125 M 0.9986 of the first
    post-Newtonian value, profiles within 0.98-1.06, no particle lost. With shift_eta = 2
    (250 M): separation 30 -> 26.6 and a slow phase, a coordinate effect
  - card against processor (source before the merge): t = 0 records equal to 1e-11,
    histories of v0 and v0.3 equal to 1e-14 over 10 M and 34.5 M
  - the elliptic solver (id_solve) on the moving cluster, 64 cells: converges in 24-25
    iterations (max|H| 1.2e-3 -> 2e-12) and agrees with the radial correction within 5
    per cent with its Robin faces at 16 M, within 3 per cent rms with the faces at 32 M
  - merged source against the source before the merge, new options on (processors):
    history files and cell data byte-identical (test M1)
