# Notation of the F-hills working note

Units G = c = 1; lengths and times in units of the heavy black hole's mass M unless stated.
Preamble macros of `note/main.tex`: `\dd` (differential), `\rt` (r_t), `\rp` (r_p),
`\Eu` (energy unit E_u), `\mb` (m_b).

| Symbol | Meaning | First use |
|---|---|---|
| M | mass of the heavy black hole; unit of length and time | Sec. II |
| m_1, m_2 | masses of the two members (bare puncture masses in the code) | Sec. II |
| m_b = m_1 + m_2 | binary mass | Sec. II |
| q = M/m_b | mass ratio | Sec. II |
| r_g = M, r_gb = m_b | gravitational radii of the black hole and of the binary | Sec. II |
| a_b | semimajor axis of the binary | Sec. II |
| r_t = a_b q^(1/3) | tidal radius | Sec. II |
| r_p | pericentre of the centre-of-mass orbit (Newtonian conic of the start state) | Sec. II |
| D = r_p/r_t | penetration factor (beta = 1/D in the geodesic scan) | Sec. II |
| E_u = (m_1 m_2/a_b) q^(1/3) | energy unit of a member after separation | Sec. II |
| E | energy of a member in units of E_u | Sec. II |
| r_mb = 4M, L_mb = 4M | radius and angular momentum per unit mass of the marginally bound (parabolic) orbit | Sec. II |
| t_GW | Peters merger time of the binary | Sec. II |
| Gamma | Lorentz-like factor of the geodesic baseline (pair force divided by Gamma^2) | Sec. III |
| v_infinity | ejection speed at infinity | Sec. IV |
| m/8, m/16 | finest cell width in units of the member mass | Sec. III |

Names used instead of equation numbers (3.4): "the tidal radius", "the plunge bound"
(a_b/r_gb > 4 q^(2/3)), "the geodesic baseline" (exact Schwarzschild acceleration of each
member plus the Newtonian pair force), "the energy unit".

Overlaps kept on purpose: none. The geodesic scan's beta (= 1/D) is the published
parameter of that scan and is used only in its figure and subsection.
