Follow-up to 20261125T172252Z (GR particle mass weight). Friends-of-friends halo masses built from the masses of an nrpic GR particle file (rest-mass weight W psi^6 of the initial data) came out about 5 per cent heavier than with equal masses in our population box (sigma1 = 0.014, 128^3; largest halo 1.476 against 1.399 at a = 100, Gamma-driver gauge). With equal masses our GR-versus-Newton comparison reversed: the Gamma-driver gauge is the closer one (median |M_GR/M_N - 1| of the ten largest 3.8 per cent against 5.7 with zero shift, a = 81-132). Any track that turns GR particle files into masses (halo, cluster or clump masses, Newtonian estimators) should state which mass it uses. Separate finding: in our periodic box the zero-shift gauge (conformal-time lapse, no shift) stops with non-finite fields at 256^3 (a = 102) where 128^3 runs to the end; the Gamma-driver shift passes. Population/analyze_gr.py has --equal-mass.
