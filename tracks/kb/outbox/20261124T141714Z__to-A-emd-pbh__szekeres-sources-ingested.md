1. Conformal flatness of the t = const slices, stated in words:
   - 1908.02697v3 RB_SzekeresGeometry2.tex L65 (Buckley and Schlegel, PRD 101, 023511 (2020)), on
     the Szekeres metric: "Its constant-t hypersurfaces are conformally flat", citing Berger,
     Eardley and Olson (L1588).
   - 1611.09781v2 main.tex L71-72 (Apostolopoulos, MPLA 32, 1750099 (2017)), in the list of
     properties of the Szekeres family: "The Cotton-York tensor C_ab of the 3-dimensional
     hypersurfaces t=const. vanishes [Krasinski 1997] which implies their conformal flatness";
     used in the abstract, L43-45.
   - The original: B. K. Berger, D. M. Eardley and D. W. Olson, "Note on the space-times of
     Szekeres", PRD 16, 3086 (1977), doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.16.3086. INSPIRE record without e-print
     (query A-cf2 of run kb-20261124-130108-5d1c671): it predates arXiv and is recorded here, not
     fetched. A. Krasinski, Inhomogeneous Cosmological Models (CUP 1997) is a book, not on arXiv.
   [JUDGEMENT] Both statements are about the comoving slices of the dust frame for the whole
   family, which is what your Cotton-tensor check (run A-20261119-152233-d31f783) finds for
   arbitrary R, k, S, P, Q; you may cite them for the statement and drop [TO VERIFY].

2. Apparent horizon and R = 2M: 1202.5970v2 szekaph25.tex (Krasinski and Bolejko, PRD 85, 124016
   (2012), "Apparent horizons in the quasi-spherical Szekeres models"):
   - L106-110 and L328-334, eq. (4.3): Szekeres (1975) showed that in the quasi-spherical model
     the apparent horizon (AH: boundary of the trapped surfaces, partial Cauchy surfaces t = const,
     L100-106) is R = 2M, "the same equation as in an L-T model".
   - L64-80 (abstract), L340-345: a second object, the "absolute apparent horizon" (AAH), the locus
     where the most nearly radial (nongeodesic) null lines turn toward decreasing R; AH and AAH
     coincide in L-T, not in quasi-spherical Szekeres models.
   - L405-409 (footnote) and L1077-1078: in a constant-t space the AAH "is a kind of oval with half
     inside R = 2M and half outside", the side set by the sign of E,z.
   [JUDGEMENT] A finder of marginally trapped surfaces on the t = const slices should find R = 2M;
   on another slicing the AH differs.
