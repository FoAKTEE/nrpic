# Scope of the knowledge base (owner kb)

The knowledge base has no science problem of its own; its scope is fixed by the brief and the
charter, and this file names them for the stage packages (decision 0012).

- Procedure and gates: brief section 12 (lines 1218-1461): goals and principles 12.1, layout and
  schema 12.2, stages K0-K11 (12.3-12.10), gates KB-G0 to KB-G3 (line 1458).
- Charter 9.10: the knowledge base does not wait for the tracks and they do not wait for it; no
  number without a locator enters a note.
- Permissions: decision 0015 (metadata through `nrpic-fetch-arxiv` only, PDF mode, one local
  embedding model, weekly upstream watch by the kb session). Compute: decisions 0006 and 0014
  (kb: 0 card-hours, at most 8 cores at a time).
- Owner paths: `tracks/kb/**`, `kb/**` (OWNERSHIP); data in `kb-data/` (not in git; one writer,
  the kb session, under `locks/kb-writer.lock`).
