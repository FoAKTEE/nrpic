# 0050 Track B: gate G2 held until the late-time strain is settled

Status: ACCEPTED · 2026-11-27 · orchestrator (decision 0017)

The allotments stand.

1. The independent review of Track B's G2 recommends a pass with reservations and says
   on what it depends: the item on three-way convergence is met for the band strain of
   the halo box up to a = 49 and not after (the two gauges differ by 5, 20 and 36 per
   cent at a = 64, 100 and 132; the particle-number leg exists only with zero shift),
   and whether that item covers the whole range is the orchestrator's reading.
   Ruling: it covers the whole range the track's science uses; a strain verified only
   before most of the structure has formed is not the capability G3 needs. The gate is
   held, not failed: what settles it is cheap and named by the review.
2. Before the signature, either or both, each by a run or analysis that could fail:
   (a) the late band strain compared between the two gauges in a measure that does not
   depend on the gauge (the Weyl scalar, or the review's other proposal), from the
   dumps that exist; (b) the member of 128 cells at eight particles per cell with the
   Gamma-driver shift (0.85 card-hours, the initial data exist), read against the
   member at one particle per cell in the same gauge. The member of 256 cells at eight
   per cell (two to three days) is not waited for.
3. Then a second review limited to that item signs or not. The corrections the review
   lists are made meanwhile, and the scope text gets, with the date, the two things it
   leaves unplaced: its clause on the truncation error of the first source across
   three meshes, and which gauge leg G3 means (the scope says the lapse condition; the
   package varied the shift).
4. Recorded from the review: the track's own criterion on the expanding background
   passes by two orders of magnitude with an estimator independent of the lead's; the
   inference that streams cross in the shearing-dust case as run conflicts with the
   reference paper, where the codes that report a number at that amplitude are
   particle codes: the cause of that failure is open again.
5. Added after batch 39 (2026-11-27 20:20 PST). Condition (a) was measured and is not
   met: a reader of the magnetic part of the Weyl tensor, tested on an analytic wave
   and unmoved by three families of coordinate maps, still differs between the two
   gauges on slices that agree (median ratio 0.94 and 0.82 at a = 100 and 132, against
   0.80 and 0.64 for the band strain). Most of the gauge dependence of the late strain
   is therefore a coordinate effect, and up to about a tenth to a fifth is not. The
   gate stays held; condition (b), the member at eight particles per cell in the
   shifted gauge (queue entry q0107), decides whether the remainder falls with the
   sampling. The second review follows its reading.
