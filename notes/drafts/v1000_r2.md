# Numerical-relativity particle-in-cell method at low particle number: An independent reconstruction by language-model agents

Dense star clusters and dark-matter spikes near black holes evolve under collective gravity. They obey the Einstein-Vlasov system, where collisionless matter follows geodesics of the curvature its energy and momentum generate. Wu and Most modeled it with a numerical-relativity particle-in-cell method using millions of phase-space packets called macroparticles. Does their paper suffice for an independent rebuild from text alone? Which strong-field results survive at ten thousand macroparticles, where particle updates cost little beside spacetime evolution, and what changes?

Language-model agents under human direction rebuilt the code from text alone in AthenaK. One agent wrote derivations and code, peers built independent reference solutions and analyzed runs, and a third wrote the manuscript. Every diagnosis was tested against a prediction written before a falsifying control run. This rule overturned several first explanations. Because collisionless phase-space density is constant along geodesics, macroparticles in six phase-space variables, position and spatial momentum, sample matter and source curvature. Each step runs a particle-mesh loop where AthenaK's constraint-damped moving-puncture evolution advances spatial slices like a Maxwell solver. The lapse relates proper to coordinate time, and the shift tracks coordinate drift relative to slice-normal observers. A geodesic pusher updates macroparticle positions and momenta, and deposition spreads their energy and momentum onto the mesh.

Two second-order pushers advance macroparticles in the interpolated metric. The implicit midpoint pusher evaluates coordinate geodesic equations at the midpoint, conserves stationary symmetries to rounding error, and reproduces every published Kerr digit. The spacetime Boris pusher adapts the electromagnetic plasma update, with two half-step electric-like kicks around an energy-preserving magnetic-like rotation. In a local orthonormal frame of normal observers, metric gradients drive the kicks while turning frame axes produce the rotation. The spatial metric fixes that frame up to a local rotation called the tetrad gauge. Discrete midpoint steps differ across frames at second order, like stepping an orbit in rotating polar unit vectors versus fixed Cartesian ones. Wu and Most leave the tetrad gauge unstated. On a near-capture Kerr passage, with Schwarzschild as the nonrotating limit, the symmetric gauge from the metric square root matches their analytic digits. The Cholesky gauge from sequential orthogonalization matches their mesh digits, where four-point cubic Hermite interpolation sets the error scale while the frame shifts its coefficient.

Each macroparticle deposits ten normal-observer source components, energy density, momentum density, and spatial stress, via a piecewise-linear cloud-in-cell shape function over eight receiving cells. On an octree mesh where refined blocks split cells into eight children, local-cell clouds would mismatch at coarse-fine boundaries and break conservation. Each macroparticle therefore uses one representation level on a virtual grid: coarser cells sum weights, and finer blocks split weights evenly among children. Dividing by proper volume preserves the conservation identity: mesh integrals equal particle sums to rounding error across regridding and level crossings. Tracers around fixed and evolving black holes keep invariants, phase-mix into rings, and feel the slice mass. Lapse excision removes plunging tracers at the exact threshold once collapsed lapse inside a black hole freezes coordinate motion. Comparison of apparent horizons, outermost closed surfaces of non-expanding light rays, with the geometric Misner-Sharp mass of the slice exposed a defect in AthenaK's apparent-horizon finder. Unfilled ghost cells in a derivative array biased masses low where mesh-block faces cut the surface, worse at finer spacing. Differentiating the metric interpolant restores agreement within the accuracy of the mass measure.

Self-gravity at ten thousand macroparticles exposes three sparse-ensemble effects, as sampling noise scales with the inverse square root of macroparticles per cell and refinement divides cell counts by eight. First, spherical dust on a lattice with the published cell-volume mass rule carries a discrete surface excess negligible for millions of particles. At ten thousand macroparticles that excess makes the apparent horizon heavy, and outer-layer rescaling restores agreement at formation within the accuracy of the mass measure. Second, suppressing noise needs several binomial passes with weights one-quarter, one-half, and one-quarter, yet each plain pass widens clouds and softens gravity. Stable clusters then bounce outward by a visible fraction of their radius, and unstable ones expand earlier. A sharpening compensation pass from plasma simulation cancels cloud widening and removes the bounce while keeping smoothing. Third, level-capped deposition keeps clouds on a coarser grid when cells shrink below interparticle spacing, which smooths diffuse sources and leaves central collapse unchanged. Near a forming black hole, however, even one coarsening level worsens constraints and drives apparent-horizon mass drift, so finest-level deposition stays.

Particle-independent tests hold at full precision: initial slices at rest, monoenergetic cluster equilibria of equal-energy stars, and early dust collapse. In pressureless free fall, interior density follows the exact cycloid, and central lapse follows its analytical curve until a coordinate wave arrives from the surface. Dust then forms a black hole, lapse excision removes all matter, and the apparent-horizon mass matches the total mass. Along the cluster sequence, binding energy peaks at a binding-energy maximum that separates stable equilibria from collapse. A stable cluster stays bound and centered over many dynamical times while its central lapse oscillates at the core period with growing amplitude. Controls across random seeds, one thousand to ten thousand macroparticles, time step, constraint damping, relaxation, and cloud width leave that growth unchanged.
