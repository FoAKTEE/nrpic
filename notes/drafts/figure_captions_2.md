# Captions for the migration and near-critical figures (to be edited by the writer)

Both are one-column figures (`width=\columnwidth`). Each caption was set in the paper's
document class next to its figure and takes five lines with a two-digit reference
number. The keys and line labels sit inside the figures, so the captions do not repeat
the colours.

fig_migration (column):

\caption{Unperturbed, the unstable cluster $y_c = 0.5$ migrates as in
Ref.~\cite{Wu:2026nrpic}: it expands, recontracts and oscillates about a more extended
state. Top: central lapse, equilibrium (black), extrema read from
Ref.~\cite{Wu:2026nrpic} (circles; bars: ranges read). Bottom: radii enclosing 90, 50
and 10 percent of the rest mass.}

(The text must add, or put in the Supplemental Material, these numbers from the 2182 M
read: the first maximum of the lapse is 0.913 at 228 M. The first minimum has two dips,
0.591 at 400 M and 0.541 at 437 M, which is earlier and deeper than the original's 0.57
to 0.60 at 450 to 520 M. The second maximum is 0.894 at 631 M and the second minimum
0.753 at 783 M (original: 680 and 830 M). After that the lapse oscillates between 0.79
and 0.85. After 900 M the 90 percent radius lies between 19 and 26 M (3.5 M at the
start) and the half-mass radius between 4.8 and 6.9 M (2.3 M at the start). Two of the 10^4 particles escape and none is removed.)

fig_nearcritical (column):

\caption{Compressed by ten percent in density, the near-critical cluster $y_c = 0.756$
collapses to a black hole; expanded, it oscillates and keeps every macroparticle. Top:
central lapse; band, mean lapse of Ref.~\cite{Wu:2026nrpic}; dashed, first apparent
horizon. Bottom: radii enclosing 90 and 10 percent of the rest mass.}

(The text must add that the perturbation is homologous and that the metric solves the
Hamiltonian constraint. It must also add these numbers: first apparent horizon at

run oscillates between lapse 0.72 and 0.83 about a mean of 0.78 over the run (0.776

the one that oscillates about the original's band: lapse between 0.72 and 0.77, mean
0.750, extrema 45 to 55 M apart, swing growing.)
