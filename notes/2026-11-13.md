# 2026-11-13

## Decisions
- Geodesic pushers live in one header that sees the spacetime only through a metric
  functor; the same kernels run standalone, with analytic metrics, on a virtual lattice,
  and on mesh data.
- Tetrad gauge of the spacetime Boris pusher is selectable. The published analytic
  (spherical Kerr-Schild) invariant errors are reproduced by the symmetric square-root
  gauge; the published Cartesian-grid errors by the lower Cholesky gauge in (x, y, z)
  order. Neither gauge reproduces both sets.
- Ownership and deposition use a search over MeshBlocks (single rank). Deposition is
  conservative on uniform, SMR and AMR meshes by construction.
- With at most 10^4 particles the published AMR deposition (cloud at the finest local
  level) makes the source granular once the mesh is finer than the particle spacing.
  Added a cap on the representation level (`deposit_max_level`); with the cap inactive
  the scheme is the published one.
- AthenaK requires an even number of ghost zones with mesh refinement, so refined runs
  use nghost = 4 (sixth-order differences) or 2 (second-order); the original quotes
  fourth order.

## Checks
- Kerr-Schild e = 0.99 passage, dt = 0.01 M, t = 230 M: midpoint analytic E 2.781e-8,
  L_z 0, Q 1.254e-7; Boris (symmetric) analytic 9.046e-8, 1.136e-8, 7.825e-7; midpoint
  lattice 2.162e-6, 5.304e-6, 5.060e-5; Boris (Cholesky) lattice 2.046e-6, 3.788e-6,
  5.289e-5. All agree with the published values to the digits given there.
- Mesh path (Schwarzschild, dx = 0.25 M, circular orbit r = 8 M, 300 M): E to 8e-7,
  L_z to 5e-6; deposition identity to 1e-15 over 27 MeshBlocks.
- Dust collapse, 9328 lattice particles, dx = h = 0.303 M static: central lapse follows
  the lapse bound to 1e-4 until tau_c = 2.6 M and leaves it at 3.3-3.5 M; central
  density follows the cycloid to 2e-4 before that and within 8 percent after (lattice
  aliasing).
- Z4c throughput on one GPU: 2.0e7 zone-cycles/s (nghost 4), 3.5e7 (nghost 2); particle
  work adds about 3 percent at 10^4 particles.
