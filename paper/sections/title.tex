\title{Numerical-relativity particle-in-cell method at low particle number:\\ An independent reconstruction by language-model agents}

\noaffiliation

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We ask whether the numerical-relativity particle-in-cell method of Wu and Most can be rebuilt from its published description alone and what survives at ten thousand macroparticles. Language-model agents under human direction wrote an independent implementation inside the public AthenaK code. Every test compares against exact solutions or published numbers, with explanations checked against predictions written before dedicated control runs. Single-particle geodesic benchmarks match all published digits once the unstated local-frame tetrad gauge of the spacetime Boris pusher is fixed. Unfiltered cloud-in-cell deposition conserves matter sources across mesh refinement boundaries. An independent geometric mass measure of the slice exposed a ghost-zone defect in the host code's apparent-horizon finder, corrected with derivatives of the metric interpolant. Low particle number requires two algorithmic changes: rescaled surface masses on cubic lattices and a sharpening compensation pass for multi-pass source filters. Dust collapse, prolate collapse, the sequence of relativistic clusters, and the migration of an unstable cluster are reproduced in kind, with documented differences in timing. Two overdensities collapse to black holes that spiral inward and merge in kind inside a cluster. The outcome after merger depends on the envelope radius, where the published radius and orbital speed conflict. These results show that the method is usable from its published description at the computational cost of one graphics card.
\end{abstract}
